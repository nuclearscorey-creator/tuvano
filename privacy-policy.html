Privacy Policy — Tuvano

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Tuvano (“the app”) is a media player for Windows, published by Scorey (Chongwan Wanlo Conrad). It does not provide any channels, playlists, movies, series, or program-guide content — you supply your own legally authorized sources. This policy covers both editions: the one from the Microsoft Store and the one downloaded as Tuvano-Setup.exe. It explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

  • We do not collect, sell, or share any personal data.
  • Everything you enter stays on your device.
  • There is no analytics, telemetry, tracking, or advertising.
  • The app connects only to the servers in the playlists and provider accounts you add (and, in the downloadable edition, to an update address when one is in use — see below).

What the app stores (locally, on your device)

  • Playlists and source URLs (M3U / Xtream) you add.
  • Provider credentials (username/password) you enter, stored encrypted using the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI), tied to your Windows user account.
  • App settings (language, theme, playback preferences).
  • Local viewing history and favorites you create.
  • Downloadable edition only: trial and license state (the start date of the free trial and the license key you activate), kept in the Windows registry of this PC. License keys are checked offline.

All of the above is kept on your PC. None of it is sent to the developer or any third party.

Purchases

In the Microsoft Store edition, the purchase and the free trial are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft’s own privacy statement. The publisher does not receive your payment details. The app itself does not ask for any payment information.

Network connections

The app makes network requests to the servers named in the playlists and provider accounts you add: to play streams, show channel logos and posters, and read your provider’s program guide. Your credentials are sent solely to your own provider, exactly as required to play the content you are entitled to.

Update check (downloadable edition only). When the publisher provides an update address, the downloadable edition checks for a newer version by requesting a small file from that address a few seconds after it starts and once a day while it stays open. The request carries only the app’s version number, as its user agent; no identifier, account, or usage data is sent. When checks are in use they can be turned off on the app’s About & licenses page. The Microsoft Store edition is updated by the Store and makes no such request.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins to any developer service.
  • No advertising identifiers, cookies, fingerprinting, or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.
  • No bypassing of DRM, geo-blocks, paywalls, or content protections.

Data retention & deletion

Because data never leaves your device, you are in full control. Removing a playlist in the app deletes its saved credentials and imported items.

  • Microsoft Store edition: uninstalling the app from Windows removes the data it stored.
  • Downloadable edition: uninstalling keeps your library and settings unless you tick “Also delete my playlists, history, settings and saved logins” when asked to confirm, so that reinstalling or updating does not lose them. Ticking that box deletes them from your PC.

Children’s privacy

The app is a general-audience media player and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
